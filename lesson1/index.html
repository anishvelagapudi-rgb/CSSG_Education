Represent the board

Use a two-dimensional array. Each cell is an object with a row, column, mine, nearby count, revealed, and flagged value.

Write a neighbors function: check the eight surrounding positions, but skip coordinates outside the board. Place mines, then count neighbors.

Render. Reveal. Finish a round.

Build in small steps: draw buttons from cell data; connect clicks; reveal a safe cell or end the game on a mine; expand zero cells to show their neighbors.
